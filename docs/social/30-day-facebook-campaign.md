### Day 8 — Seed Saving Tip

Before you buy seeds next spring, ask yourself one question:

Did I save any from this year?

Heirloom seeds saved and replanted correctly will outperform commercial seeds in your specific soil and climate within 3–5 generations. They adapt to you.

### Day 11 — Foraging Safety Note

Foraging is one of the most valuable skills you can develop. It's also one of the most misunderstood.

The rule is simple: if you are not 100% certain of the identification, you do not eat it. Not 90%. Not 95%. One hundred.

The Foraging library at A1 Homestead Hub covers identification by season and region, look-alikes to avoid, ethical harvesting practices, and how to build your identification skills over time — starting with the easiest and most distinctive plants before moving to anything with dangerous look-alikes.

Knowledge before harvest. Always.

## Scheduling Instructions — Meta Business Suite (Free)

Meta Business Suite is Facebook's own free scheduling tool. It works for both Facebook and Instagram simultaneously.

**Step 1 — Access Meta Business Suite**

Go to [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com) and log in with the same account that manages your A1 Homestead Hub Facebook page. If prompted, select your page from the dropdown.

**Step 2 — Open the Planner**

In the left sidebar, click **Planner**. This shows a calendar view of all your scheduled and published posts.

**Step 3 — Create a Scheduled Post**

Click the blue **Create Post** button in the top right. A composer window will open.

- Select your **Facebook Page** (A1 Homestead Hub) as the destination
- Paste the post text from this document
- Add an image if desired (the site's hero image or any homesteading photo works well)
- Click the dropdown arrow next to the blue **Publish** button
- Select **Schedule**
- Choose your date and time (recommended: 8–10am your local time)
- Click **Schedule**

**Step 4 — Repeat for All 30 Posts**

Work through this document from Day 1 to Day 30, scheduling each post one day apart. The entire process for all 30 posts takes approximately 45–60 minutes if done in one sitting.

**Step 5 — Monitor Engagement**

After each post goes live, check back within 24 hours to respond to any comments. Facebook's algorithm rewards pages that respond to comments quickly — even a simple "Thank you!" or a follow-up question keeps the post circulating.

**Pro Tip:** Pin Day 1 (the launch announcement) to the top of your page immediately after it posts. Pinned posts stay at the top of your page profile and are the first thing any visitor sees.
