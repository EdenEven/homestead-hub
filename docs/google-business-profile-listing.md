## Section 2: Location & Service Area

Because A1 Homestead Hub is a digital platform, you have two options:

**Option A — Online Business Only (Recommended to start)**
Select "I deliver goods and services to customers" and choose "I serve customers at their location." Set your service area to the United States. This keeps your home address private while still appearing in Google Search and Maps.

**Option B — Physical Address (If you want local search visibility)**
Use your home address or a P.O. box. This puts you on the map pin in local search results, which is valuable for reaching homesteaders in your specific region. Your address can be hidden from the public listing — Google only uses it to verify your location.

**Recommendation:** Start with Option A. Once you have consistent traffic and reviews, add a physical address to unlock the local map pack results.

## Section 10: First Review Strategy

Google Business Profile reviews are the single most important factor in local search ranking. Here is how to get your first five reviews without violating Google's policies:

**Who to ask:**
- Family members who have used the site
- Early community members who signed up
- Homeschool parents you know personally
- Anyone who has attended a local event you promoted through the site

**What to send them (copy-paste this message):**

> "Hey — I've been building a free homesteading platform called A1 Homestead Hub and I just set up our Google Business Profile. If you've had a chance to look at the site and found it useful, I'd really appreciate a quick honest review. It helps other homesteaders find us. Here's the link: [your Google Business Profile review link]. Thank you."

**What not to do:** Do not offer incentives for reviews. Do not ask people who have not actually used the site. Do not post fake reviews. Google detects all of these and will remove your profile.

## Section 11: Verification

After submitting your profile, Google will ask you to verify your business. For an online business, the options are typically:

1. **Phone verification** — Google calls or texts a code to your number. Fastest option.
2. **Email verification** — Google sends a code to your business email. Use nikki@a1homesteadhub.com or your primary contact email.
3. **Video verification** — Google asks you to record a short video showing your workspace. This is becoming more common for online businesses.

Complete verification within 7 days of receiving the prompt. Unverified profiles do not appear in search results.
