<text>
**Idea 1: Rugged Americana Craft**

- **Design Movement:** American Arts & Crafts / Prairie Style
- **Core Principles:** Handmade warmth, honest materials, functional beauty, community rootedness
- **Color Philosophy:** Deep forest green (#2D4A22), warm barn red (#8B2E2E), aged parchment (#F5EDD6), charcoal ink (#1C1C1C). Colors evoke the land itself — soil, timber, harvest.
- **Layout Paradigm:** Asymmetric editorial layout. Wide left-anchored hero with diagonal text blocks. Skills grid uses staggered card heights like a barn wall of tools.
- **Signature Elements:** Woodgrain texture overlays on section dividers; hand-drawn style icon set; rope/twine decorative borders
- **Interaction Philosophy:** Deliberate, satisfying — like turning a page in a field guide. Hover reveals feel like unfolding a map.
- **Animation:** Slow fade-in on scroll (300ms ease-out). Cards lift with a subtle shadow on hover. No flashy transitions — everything feels grounded.
- **Typography System:** Playfair Display (headings, bold, editorial) + Source Serif 4 (body, readable, warm). Hierarchy: 48px display / 28px section / 16px body.
</text>

**Idea 2: Field Manual / Tactical Naturalist**

- **Design Movement:** Brutalist Naturalism / Military Field Guide
- **Core Principles:** No-nonsense utility, information density, survival-ready clarity, zero decoration for decoration's sake
- **Color Philosophy:** Olive drab (#6B7C4B), raw canvas (#E8DFC8), deep earth (#3D2B1F), safety orange (#D4622A) as accent. This is a working tool, not a gallery.
- **Layout Paradigm:** Grid-based but dense — like a field manual or topo map. Navigation uses tab-style section markers. Content is organized in numbered modules.
- **Signature Elements:** Topo map contour lines as background texture; numbered section badges; bold uppercase labels on every card
- **Interaction Philosophy:** Functional first. Filters, toggles, and search dominate. Every click should accomplish something.
- **Animation:** Minimal — slide-in from left on page transitions. Accordion-style skill expansions. No decorative motion.
- **Typography System:** Space Grotesk (headings, technical, modern) + IBM Plex Serif (body, legible, structured). All caps for labels and nav items.
</text>

**Idea 3: Homestead Revival — Warm Editorial**

- **Design Movement:** Contemporary Farmhouse / Slow Living Editorial
- **Core Principles:** Welcoming and approachable, layered warmth, community-first, knowledge as heritage
- **Color Philosophy:** Warm cream (#FAF5EB), deep moss green (#3A5A40), golden amber (#C8882A), slate brown (#4A3728). Feels like a warm kitchen and a trusted almanac.
- **Layout Paradigm:** Full-bleed hero with overlapping content cards. Skills section uses a magazine-style masonry grid. Community section uses a map-centric layout with sidebar cards.
- **Signature Elements:** Leaf/branch botanical motifs as section dividers; warm amber glow on featured cards; circular profile avatars for community members
- **Interaction Philosophy:** Inviting and exploratory — like browsing a well-loved almanac. Users discover content naturally through visual hierarchy.
- **Animation:** Gentle upward fade on scroll (framer-motion). Cards have warm amber border glow on hover. Map pins pulse softly.
- **Typography System:** Lora (display headings, serif, heritage feel) + Inter (body, clean, accessible). Large pull-quotes in Lora italic for featured content.
</text>

## CHOSEN APPROACH: Idea 1 — Rugged Americana Craft

Deep forest green, barn red, aged parchment. Playfair Display headings. Asymmetric editorial layout. Feels like a trusted field guide come to life on screen.
